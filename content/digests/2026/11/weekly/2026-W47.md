This regulatory framework development validates certification infrastructure as critical complement to voluntary carbon markets — where high-integrity credit markets help bridge multi-year transition financing gaps but require systematic verification frameworks ensuring credibility at scale.

MRV infrastructure financing emerges as prerequisite for carbon market scaling confidence — particularly for forest carbon accounting where future market confidence rebuilding hinges on digital MRV advancement addressing verification gaps that undermined earlier project integrity.
